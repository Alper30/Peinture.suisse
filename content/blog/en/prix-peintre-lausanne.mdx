Planning a painting project in Lausanne and wondering what budget to expect? Here's an honest guide to prices in French-speaking Switzerland — and, more importantly, to what makes a quote vary.

## Indicative price ranges

Every job site is unique, but here are orders of magnitude commonly seen in the region:

- **Interior wall painting**: roughly 25 to 45 CHF per m² (preparation included)
- **Ceilings**: often slightly more expensive than walls, because of overhead work
- **Façade render**: highly variable depending on the condition of the surface and access — an on-site assessment is essential
- **Hourly rate for a qualified painter**: generally between 60 and 90 CHF per hour

These figures are **indicative**: only a quote drawn up after a site visit reflects the real price of your project.

## What makes the price vary

### 1. The condition of the surfaces

This is factor number one. Sound, smooth walls repaint quickly. Cracked, damp or flaking surfaces require real preparation work: filling, sanding, priming. That preparation often represents most of the working time — and it's what guarantees a lasting result.

### 2. Height and access

Stairwells, high ceilings, façades: anything that requires scaffolding or specific equipment affects the price.

### 3. The quality of the paint

Quality professional paint lasts longer, covers better and cleans more easily. The savings on a budget product are often paid back in extra coats — or in redoing the work two years later.

### 4. The level of finishing

Crisp lines between two colours, woodwork, radiators, frames: careful details take time, and that's precisely what distinguishes craftsmanship.

## How to compare quotes properly

1. **Check what's included**: surface preparation, protection of floors and furniture, the number of coats and final clean-up should be spelled out in black and white.
2. **Beware of abnormally low prices**: they often hide rushed preparation or extras appearing mid-project.
3. **Ask for an itemised quote, line by line**: a serious professional has nothing to hide.
4. **Look at past work**: before/after photos say more than long promises.
